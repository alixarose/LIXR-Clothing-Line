About Lixr

Wear less noise.
Keep more character.

Lixr is built around a simple idea: the clothes you use most should feel intentional without feeling overworked.

We focus on balanced proportions, tactile fabrics and a repeatable palette so pieces work beyond a single outfit or season. The result is a wardrobe that looks composed while staying practical for a real day.
